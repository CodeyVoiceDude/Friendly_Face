Stirred in your slumber, or is it yours? someone else’s? Does it matter? You are walking down a pathway in the woods, the canopy above you preventing any of the faint glow of the moon from shining down into the undergrowth you tread through. You can hear leaves crunch under your shoes. You come upon a small cabin, and creep inside. The inside of the cabin is large, musky too. The act of you shutting the door causes possibly hundreds of years of dust to come loose and shake from their spots in the roof. There’s a bed here, it feels familiar. It feels like it should be comfy, and yet you find yourself not laying in it. Looking around, you notice a hallway that you somehow didn’t notice before, impossibly long and yet standing just as impossibly real in front of you. Your legs move on their own, and you begin to slowly step into the hallway, darkness from the unlit hallway swallowing you whole and nestling you in.

You have spent what feels like years in these halls, every time you believe you come to the end of the hallway, your body turns and finds itself opening a side door to another hallway. By now, you estimate you’ve walked telons in distance and yet find no end to your trek. Your teeth chatter, your bones quake, you feel as though you might pass out.
You come to an end, another end, a new beginning to another hallway. Yet, as you turn to face the door, you are greeted by a tall door ornately carved with symbols that make your eyes hurt. Eyes? You almost forget you had them under your face. Your sight focuses on the symbols, they hurt, and yet you must look. You must. Black, blackness in no end, sharp angles stabbing the corners of your sight as you see it. It's a pyramid, upside down, pointing to the symbol beneath it, another, similar, black pyramid. Your vision swirls as you observe the entire frame of the door, the pyramid, it keeps going, it doesn’t end, until.
You wake up.
Standing up, you hear a leaf stuck in your boot make a satisfying crunch as you put your weight down.
